Videos are converted on the Asset Manager to the codec chosen here, by their codec. Originals are kept; converted copies go in its Optimized folder, with the same names. More chunks let a Runner unpack a frame on more CPU cores at once; without Snappy there is nothing to unpack, but files are about twice as large.
